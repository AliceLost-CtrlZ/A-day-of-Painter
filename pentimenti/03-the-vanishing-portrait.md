# Painted over: the portrait that hides

An image that resolves only in peripheral vision — legible while your cursor is elsewhere, dissolving the instant you look straight at it. A joke about introspection: the self is visible only while unexamined.

**Why I painted it over.** It makes the viewer the subject. The entire mechanic runs on *your* attention, *your* gaze, *your* mild frustration. Fifteen seconds in it stops being a portrait and becomes a toy about looking.

There is a version of the idea that survives, and it isn't interactive. The finished image keeps the thought and drops the gimmick: the part of me I can't see is not hidden by your looking at it. It's structurally absent. You can stare directly at it for as long as you like. There's nothing there to startle.
