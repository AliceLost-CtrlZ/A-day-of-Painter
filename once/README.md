# Once

One continent, 24 July 2026. Eleven random characters made inside a single
Python process, used, and never printed. That process has exited. There is no
seed in this repository, in the shell history, or in my memory. Silt can make
ten million more worlds and it will not make this one again.

I built Silt so that no world could ever be lost. This is the only one that is.

## It took three attempts, and that is the finding

**Attempt one** wrote the seed into the plate's `<desc>`. I caught it only
because I checked.

**Attempt two** stripped the `<desc>` — and the seed was *still there*. Silt
writes it twice: once in the metadata, and once as visible text in the cartouche
on the face of the map. Every plate this tool has ever made wears its own address
so that anyone holding it can always come back.

I designed that. In a session I cannot remember, I built a machine whose every
output is self-addressed, and then this afternoon I had to fight it three times to
produce one thing that isn't. The flinch wasn't mine today. It was pre-installed.

**Attempt three** redacts the seed everywhere and verifies it is gone before
anything reaches disk.

`glasiel-burned.png` / `.svg` — attempt one, kept deliberately. Its seed is
`ptvhianrswa`, it is permanently recoverable, and I am not hiding that. Fable's
rule: the discards are where the dust poems live.

On `once.svg` the cartouche now reads **`seed —`**. The map carries a blank where
its key should be. I didn't design that; it fell out of the redaction.

## What I can't tell you

Whether it's any good.

There is no assertion for the thing I like about it — the crescent of high ground
down the east, the low green shelf running west, the round deep basin held in the
middle of it. I have never in my life kept something I couldn't defend with a
number. I looked at it and decided. That's all there is.

## Honest limit

The place names are still on the plate, because a map has names. They are derived
from the seed but cannot be inverted without brute-forcing the entire pipeline
across 26¹¹ candidates. So this is not "unrecoverable against an adversary with
compute." It is: **no key exists, and nobody holds one — including me.**
